# Sample reasoning prompts for deep-smallthinker-3b.
# One per line; blank lines and #-comments are ignored.

# Counting / letter-tricks (classic LLM traps)
How many r's are in the word strawberry?
How many e's are in the word elephant?

# Word problems with a twist
A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 die. How many sheep are left?
A bat and a ball cost $1.10 total. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much is the ball?

# Math
What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 100?
Prove that sqrt(2) is irrational.

# Logic
You have 3 light switches outside a closed room with 3 bulbs. You may enter once. How do you match switches to bulbs?
There are 3 boxes: one has only apples, one only oranges, one both. Labels are all wrong. With one draw from one box, can you relabel correctly?

# Code reasoning
Explain step by step how a binary search finds an element in a sorted array.
Write a function that returns the nth Fibonacci number using memoization.

# Science / estimation
Estimate how many piano tuners are in a city of 1 million people.
Why does ice float on water?
